# Incident: short descriptive title

**Date:** Month and year
**Severity:** Low, Medium, or High, with one line on impact

## Summary

Two or three sentences on what happened and what was affected.

## Timeline

1. What was noticed first
2. What was checked and what it showed
3. What changed the direction of the investigation
4. What fixed it

## Root cause

What actually went wrong, stated plainly and without blame.

## What worked

Habits or tools that shortened the investigation.

## Corrective actions

| Action | Status |
| --- | --- |
| What will prevent a repeat | Done, Planned, or Ongoing |

## Lessons

Short, general takeaways that apply beyond this one incident.
